How it works

The board is a grid of squares. Each square has grass or bare ground, and may hold one animal. The board wraps around at the edges.

  • Grass. Each step, every bare square has a 4% chance to grow grass. Nothing else spreads it.
  • Energy. Every animal has an energy number. Each step it loses 1 and moves to a neighboring square. At 0 it dies.
  • Rabbits move onto grass next to them if there is any, and otherwise to any free square. Eating grass gives 6 energy. At 12 a rabbit splits in two.
  • Foxes do the same with rabbits. Eating a rabbit gives 4 energy, and a fox splits at 24.
  • The cycle. Rabbits boom, foxes follow, the rabbits run out, the foxes starve, and it starts again. Each line on the chart is scaled to its own highest point.
